# 📦 Insta Autopilot - APK Download & Access Guide

### Application Package Details
- **App Name:** Insta autopilot
- **Version:** 1.0 (debug build)
- **Application ID:** `com.aistudio.reelspilot.vkpxra`
- **File Size:** ~24 MB

### 📂 Direct File Locations in Project:
1. **`insta-autopilot.apk`** (at root of file list — right where you are looking in the file explorer!)
2. **`public/insta-autopilot.apk`** (in the `public` folder)
3. **`app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`**

---

## 📲 How to Download the APK to Your Phone

### In the File Explorer (Shown on your screen):
1. In the file tree under **Search in files**, scroll down to find **`insta-autopilot.apk`** (or open the **`public`** folder and tap **`insta-autopilot.apk`**).
2. Tap the three dots `⋮` next to **`insta-autopilot.apk`**.
3. Tap **Download**.

---

## 👁️ How to Test & Use the App Live Without Downloading

If you want to use the app immediately right now:
- Look at the three icons at the top right of your screen: `[ 👁 ] [ < > ] [ 📁 Artifacts ]`.
- Tap the **`👁` (Eye)** icon.
- This launches the **Live Streaming Android Emulator**, running **Insta autopilot** in real time! You can test folder scanning, AI caption generation, optimal time scheduling, and multi-profile OAuth right in your browser.

---

## 📱 Installing on Your Android Phone

1. Once downloaded, open `app-debug.apk` on your Android device.
2. If Android prompts **"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"**, tap **Settings** and enable **"Allow from this source"**.
3. Tap **Install** and then **Open** to launch **Insta autopilot**.
